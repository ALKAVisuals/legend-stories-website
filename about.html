Made for your space

Designed as wall art.
Not as a poster.

LegendMural designs are printed as matte vinyl wall stickers. The matte finish is intended to reduce glare and create a clean wall-art appearance.

Each design keeps its original proportions and is available in two catalogue sizes, so the artwork stays true to the composition while you choose the scale that suits your space.
